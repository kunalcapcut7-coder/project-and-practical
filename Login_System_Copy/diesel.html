Mechanic Diesel

1 Year NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹10,000–₹28,000/month

The Mechanic Diesel trade provides focused, intensive training on diesel engines used in trucks, buses, agricultural machinery, and industrial generators. Students learn complete fuel injection systems, cooling systems, and engine overhaul procedures.

Syllabus / Subjects

This is a 1-year intensive trade with a focused single-year syllabus.

  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Engineering Drawing – Diesel Engine Diagrams
  • Trade Theory – Diesel Engine Systems & Components
  • Trade Practical – Fuel Injection Systems & Overhaul
  • Cooling System & Lubrication Practical
  • Employability & Safety Skills

Future Scope & Career Path

Diesel mechanics are essential in agriculture, mining, logistics, and power generation sectors. After completing this 1-year trade, students can advance to the 2-year Mechanic Motor Vehicle trade for broader qualification. Opportunities exist with the Indian Army as Vehicle Mechanic or Engine Fitter. The growing logistics sector (trucks, delivery fleets) ensures continuous demand. Experienced diesel mechanics can open their own repair workshops specializing in commercial vehicles and generators.
