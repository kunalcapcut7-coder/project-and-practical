Architectural Assistant

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹12,000–₹40,000/month

The Architectural Assistant trade blends artistic creativity with technical precision. Students learn building design, architectural drawing, 3D visualization, and CAD tools — preparing them to work alongside architects and civil engineers in designing residential, commercial, and public buildings.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Architectural Drawing – Fundamentals & Conventions
  • Building Bye-Laws & Space Planning
  • Construction Materials & Building Methods
  • Freehand Sketching & Model Making
  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Employability Skills
  • AutoCAD 2D – Architectural Floor Plan & Elevation
  • SketchUp / 3D Visualization Basics
  • Interior Design Elements & Landscaping
  • Structural Systems in Buildings
  • Quantity Estimation & Costing
  • Project Work – Full Residential Building Design

Career Opportunities

Architectural Draftsman Interior Design Assistant Building Drawing Technician 3D Visualization Artist Real Estate Drawing Specialist Site Supervision Assistant

Placement Opportunities

Architectural Firms Real Estate Developers Interior Design Studios Construction Companies Government (AUDA / Town Planning) CAD Outsourcing Firms

Future Scope & Career Path

With India's urban housing boom and Smart Cities Mission, architectural assistants are in strong demand. Graduates with skills in SketchUp, Lumion, or Revit can work as freelancers creating 3D building visualizations for real estate clients with excellent earning potential. They can also pursue a Diploma in Architecture or lateral entry into B.Arch programs with additional qualifications. Interior design is a premium growth field where artistic graduates thrive.
