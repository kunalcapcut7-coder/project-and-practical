Plumber

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹12,000–₹40,000/month

The Plumber trade provides comprehensive training in installation, repair, and maintenance of water supply systems, drainage systems, gas pipelines, and sanitary fittings in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. Plumbers are among the most in-demand skilled tradespeople with excellent self-employment potential.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Plumbing Fundamentals – Tools & Materials
  • Pipe Identification – GI, CPVC, PVC & Copper
  • Water Supply Systems – Overhead & Underground
  • Pipe Fitting – Joints, Unions & Bending
  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Engineering Drawing – Plumbing Diagrams
  • Employability Skills
  • Sanitary System Installation – Toilets, Sinks & Drains
  • Gas Pipeline Installation & Safety
  • Hot Water System – Solar & Electric Geysers
  • Fire-Fighting Pipe Systems & Sprinklers
  • Sewage Treatment & Drainage Networks
  • Plumbing Estimation & Costing
  • Project Work – Building Plumbing Layout

Future Scope & Career Path

Plumbers are among the most in-demand tradespeople across India and internationally. With urbanization, smart city projects, and massive housing construction, qualified plumbers are always needed. Self-employment is extremely viable — an experienced plumber with a small team can earn ₹50,000–₹1,00,000+ per month. Gulf countries (UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia) actively recruit Indian plumbers with ITI certification for large infrastructure projects at 3–4x the domestic salary.
