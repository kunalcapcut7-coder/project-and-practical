Data Entry Operator

1 Year NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹10,000–₹25,000/month

The Data Entry Operator trade focuses on developing fast and accurate keyboard skills, proficiency in MS Office applications, spreadsheet management, and digital data processing. Graduates enter a wide range of industries — from government offices and banks to healthcare and logistics.

Syllabus / Subjects

Intensive 1-year program with speed and accuracy benchmarks at every stage.

  • Computer Fundamentals & Keyboard Basics
  • English Typing – Speed Building (40+ WPM target)
  • Hindi Typing – Mangal/Kruti Dev Fonts
  • MS Word – Document Formatting & Mail Merge
  • MS Excel – Data Tables, Formulas & Pivot Tables
  • MS PowerPoint – Presentations & Slides
  • Data Processing – Forms, Registers & Reports
  • Tally ERP Basics – Accounts & Inventory Data
  • Internet, Email & Digital Communication
  • Employability & Office Communication Skills

Future Scope & Career Path

Data Entry Operators are the backbone of every office and institution. With skills in Tally, advanced Excel, or database management, graduates can grow into Accounts Assistant or MIS Executive roles with significantly higher pay. Government recruitment for data entry posts (NHM, collector offices, courts) provides stable employment across Gujarat. With additional online courses in Python or SQL, career advancement into IT or business analytics becomes possible.
