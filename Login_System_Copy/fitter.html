Fitter

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹10,000–₹30,000/month

The Fitter trade builds expertise in precision fitting, assembly, and maintenance of mechanical components and machinery. Students learn to use hand tools, measuring instruments, and perform fitting operations in manufacturing environments.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Engineering Drawing
  • Trade Theory – Fitting & Metrology
  • Trade Practical – Bench Work & Filing
  • Employability Skills
  • Advanced Fitting Theory
  • Trade Practical – Machine Assembly & Maintenance
  • Workshop Technology
  • Engineering Drawing – Assembly Drawings
  • Project Work & Industrial Visit

Future Scope & Career Path

Fitters are in continuous demand across manufacturing, aerospace, defense, and heavy industries. After gaining experience, skilled fitters can advance to Team Leader or Production Supervisor roles. Lateral entry to Diploma in Mechanical Engineering is available. Government job opportunities exist with Railways, Defense, and PSU companies. Self-employed fitters can start machine maintenance service businesses with excellent income potential.
