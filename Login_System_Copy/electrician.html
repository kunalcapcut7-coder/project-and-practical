The Electrician trade is one of the most sought-after engineering trades. It provides comprehensive training in electrical installations, wiring systems, and maintenance of equipment for both domestic and industrial applications.

Future Scope & Career Path

The Electrician trade offers excellent growth opportunities. After completing the ITI course, students can pursue NCVT certification, appear for government job exams (Railways, PWD, PGVCL), or pursue a Diploma in Electrical Engineering via lateral entry. With experience, electricians can become licensed electrical contractors, start their own business, or advance to senior technician positions in multinational companies. The growing infrastructure sector and renewable energy boom further ensure strong long-term demand for skilled electricians across India and internationally.
