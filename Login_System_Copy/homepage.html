About Our Institute

Premier Vocational Training Center in Gujarat

Established in 1957, Government Industrial Training Institute Kubernagar is a flagship vocational institute under the Gujarat Government. For over six decades, we have been nurturing technical talent aligned with evolving industry demands.

Located near Galaxy Cinema, Kubernagar, Ahmedabad — ITI Code: GU24000101. Fully approved by NCVT & GCVT.

Admission Process

1

Online Registration

Visit itiadmission.gujarat.gov.in to complete your application form (April–June).

2

Fee Payment

Pay ₹50 registration fee online. Annual fee: ₹600 (boys). Girl students are fully exempt.

3

Document Verification

Submit 10th marksheet, date of birth proof, and domicile certificate at the institute.

4

Admission Confirmed

Merit-based selection. Check the merit list and complete enrollment to secure your seat.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed 10th from a recognized board
  • Age: 14 to 40 years (relaxations as per Govt. norms)
  • Merit-based selection process
