Fashion Design & Technology

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹12,000–₹50,000/month

Fashion Design & Technology is a 2-year advanced trade that goes beyond basic stitching to cover fashion illustration, apparel design, textile science, and garment technology. Graduates are equipped for creative careers in fashion design studios, textile mills, retail fashion brands, and entrepreneurship.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Fashion Illustration – Sketching & Figure Drawing
  • Textile Science – Fibre, Yarn & Fabric Properties
  • Colour Theory & Design Elements
  • Pattern Making – Basic Bodice & Trouser Blocks
  • Garment Construction – Ladies' Wear & Western Outfits
  • Draping Techniques on Dress Form
  • Employability Skills
  • Advanced Apparel Design – Collection Design
  • Computer-Aided Fashion Design – CorelDRAW / Illustrator
  • Garment Technology – Industrial Sewing & QC
  • Embroidery – Hand, Machine & Zari Work
  • Merchandising & Retail Fashion Basics
  • Fashion Trends & Consumer Behaviour
  • Project Work – Mini Fashion Collection

Career Opportunities

Fashion Designer Fashion Illustrator Apparel Production Supervisor Boutique Owner / Designer Fashion Merchandiser Pattern Master / Grader Retail Brand Stylist

Placement Opportunities

Textile & Apparel Export Units Retail Fashion Brands (FBB, Westside) Garment Manufacturing Companies Bridal & Designer Boutiques Fashion Export Houses Film & TV Costume Departments

Future Scope & Career Path

India's fashion and textile industry is one of the world's largest, and Gujarat (Surat, Ahmedabad) is a major hub. Fashion graduates can pursue B.Des or advanced fashion design programs, or establish their own label with minimal investment. Social media platforms (Instagram, YouTube) have created massive opportunities for independent fashion designers to market their creations globally. With skills in CAD fashion design, graduates can work remotely for international fashion studios.
