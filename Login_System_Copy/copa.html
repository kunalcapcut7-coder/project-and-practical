COPA

Computer Operator & Programming Assistant

1 Year NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹10,000–₹30,000/month

COPA is one of the most popular non-engineering trades that prepares students for the digital workplace. It covers computer fundamentals, MS Office, internet, basic programming, and data management — making graduates ready for IT support, data entry, and office automation roles.

Syllabus / Subjects

Single-year intensive program covering theory and practical from day one.

  • Computer Fundamentals & Hardware Basics
  • Operating System – Windows & Linux Basics
  • MS Office – Word, Excel, PowerPoint
  • Internet, Email & Cyber Safety
  • Typing Skills – English & Hindi (30 WPM target)
  • Programming Basics – Introduction to C Language
  • Database Fundamentals – MS Access & Tally Basics
  • Web Browsing & Digital Communication
  • Employability & Office Communication Skills

Career Opportunities

Computer Operator Data Entry Operator IT Support Technician Office Automation Executive Tally Operator Back Office Executive Junior Programmer (after upskilling) E-Commerce Assistant

Placement Opportunities

IT Companies & BPOs Government Offices & Banks Hospitals & Healthcare Firms Schools & Educational Institutions Retail & E-Commerce Companies Private Corporate Offices Common Service Centres (CSC)

Future Scope & Career Path

COPA graduates have excellent growth potential in the IT sector. After gaining experience, many advance to roles in software testing, web content management, or digital marketing. Further studies like BCA, Diploma in IT, or short courses in Python/Web Development significantly boost salary prospects. Common Service Centres (CSCs) across Gujarat actively hire COPA graduates to assist citizens with government digital services, creating stable local employment opportunities.

Further Study Options

BCA / B.Sc. IT (with COPA credit) Python / Web Development Short Course Tally ERP Advanced Certification Cyber Security Basics Certificate Digital Marketing Certificate
