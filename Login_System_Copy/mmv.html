Mechanic Motor Vehicle

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹10,000–₹35,000/month

The MMV trade covers complete automobile servicing, repair, and maintenance for two-wheelers, cars, trucks, and buses. Includes modern diagnostics, electronic systems, and fundamental Electric Vehicle (EV) servicing concepts.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Engineering Drawing – Auto Components
  • Trade Theory – Engine & Transmission Systems
  • Trade Practical – Engine Dismantling & Assembly
  • Employability Skills
  • Advanced Auto Theory – Electronics & EV Basics
  • Trade Practical – Electrical Systems & AC Servicing
  • Workshop Technology – Modern Diagnostics
  • Engineering Drawing – Hydraulic Systems
  • Project Work & Garage Practice

Career Opportunities

Auto Service Technician Service Advisor Garage Owner Fleet Maintenance Technician EV Technician Dealership Mechanic

Placement Opportunities

Maruti Suzuki Tata Motors Hero MotoCorp Mahindra & Mahindra State Transport Corporations Private Garages & Dealerships

Future Scope & Career Path

With India's auto industry booming and EV adoption accelerating, MMV-trained technicians are in massive demand. Graduates can specialize in EV servicing — one of the fastest growing technical fields. Certified technicians at authorized dealer workshops earn competitive salaries with structured career growth. Entrepreneurial graduates can open their own garage or two-wheeler service center with very low startup costs.
