Turner

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹12,000–₹32,000/month

The Turner trade focuses on lathe machine operations for manufacturing precise cylindrical components. Students gain expertise in cutting, turning, boring, and threading operations on various metals used in automotive, aerospace, and heavy industries.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Engineering Drawing – 2D Profiles
  • Trade Theory – Lathe Machine Basics
  • Trade Practical – Turning & Facing
  • Employability Skills
  • Advanced Turning Theory – CNC Basics
  • Trade Practical – Threading & Boring
  • Workshop Technology – Precision Measurement
  • Engineering Drawing – Component Drawings
  • Project Work

Career Opportunities

Lathe Operator CNC Machine Operator Tool Room Technician Precision Engineering Technician Production Supervisor

Placement Opportunities

ISRO Indian Railways HAL Tata Steel Defense Ordnance Auto Component Manufacturers

Future Scope & Career Path

Turner graduates are highly sought in precision manufacturing, aerospace, and defense sectors. CNC machining skills open doors to premium-paying roles. Graduates can specialize in CNC Programming, pursue Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, or work in high-precision sectors. With experience, one can become a Tool Room Supervisor or CNC Programmer with significantly higher salaries.
