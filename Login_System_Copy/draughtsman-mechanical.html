Draughtsman Mechanical

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹12,000–₹40,000/month

Draughtsman Mechanical trains students to create detailed mechanical drawings, assembly drawings, and component designs using manual drafting techniques and advanced CAD/CAM software. This trade is the backbone of engineering product design across automotive, aerospace, and manufacturing industries.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Engineering Drawing – Projections & Sectional Views
  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Machine Drawing – Threads, Keys & Fasteners
  • Trade Theory – Machine Elements
  • Practical – Manual Drafting & Instruments
  • Employability Skills
  • AutoCAD 2D – Mechanical Component Drawing
  • SolidWorks / CATIA Basics – 3D Modelling
  • Assembly & Detail Drawings
  • Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T)
  • CAM Basics & Production Drawings
  • Project Work – Complete Machine Assembly Drawing

Career Opportunities

Mechanical Draftsman CAD Designer Product Design Technician AutoCAD Operator Manufacturing Drawing Technician Aerospace Drawing Assistant Automotive Design Draftsman

Placement Opportunities

Tata Motors / Maruti / HMSI HAL (Hindustan Aeronautics) BHEL / ISRO Machine Tool Manufacturers Engineering Consultancies Defense Equipment Manufacturers

Future Scope & Career Path

Draughtsman Mechanical graduates are highly valued in product design studios, automotive R&D centres, and manufacturing units. Learning advanced tools like SolidWorks, CATIA, or ANSYS dramatically increases career prospects and salary. Freelance CAD drafting is also a growing income opportunity. Students can pursue Diploma in Mechanical Engineering via lateral entry or take up specialized CAD/CAM certifications to move into Product Design Engineering roles.
