Draughtsman Civil

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹12,000–₹35,000/month

Draughtsman Civil is a highly technical trade that trains students to create precise architectural and civil engineering drawings — including building plans, road layouts, and structural drawings — using both manual drafting and modern CAD software like AutoCAD.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Engineering Drawing – Fundamentals
  • Building Materials & Construction Technology
  • Civil Drawing – Plans, Elevations & Sections
  • Surveying Basics & Levelling
  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Employability Skills
  • AutoCAD 2D & 3D Civil Drafting
  • Structural Drawing – RCC, Beams & Columns
  • Road, Bridge & Drainage Drawing
  • Estimation & Costing Basics
  • Site Practical & Field Survey
  • Project Work – Building Drawing

Career Opportunities

Civil Draftsman Architectural Draftsman Road & Infrastructure Designer AutoCAD Operator Site Supervisor (Junior) Municipal Corporation Draftsman Government PWD Draftsman

Placement Opportunities

Construction Companies (L&T, NCC) Government PWD / NHAI Real Estate Companies Architectural Firms Municipal Corporations (AUDA/AMC) Infrastructure Projects

Future Scope & Career Path

India's infrastructure boom — smart cities, highways, metro rails, and housing projects — has created enormous demand for skilled civil draftsmen. After gaining experience, graduates can specialize in 3D BIM (Building Information Modelling) or advance to Junior Civil Engineer with a Diploma in Civil Engineering via lateral entry. Government jobs through GPSC, SSC, and municipal corporations offer stable careers with pension benefits for qualified draftsmen.
