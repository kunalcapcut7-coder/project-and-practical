Dress Making

1 Year NCVT Certified 8th / 10th Pass ₹8,000–₹30,000/month

The Dress Making trade trains students in the complete process of garment creation — from fabric selection and body measurements to pattern making, cutting, stitching, and finishing. This trade is ideal for students interested in fashion and offers excellent self-employment potential.

Syllabus / Subjects

Hands-on 1-year program with focus on practical garment construction skills.

  • Fabric Knowledge – Types, Textures & Properties
  • Body Measurements & Size Charts
  • Pattern Making – Basic Blocks & Dart Manipulation
  • Cutting Techniques – Fabric Laying & Cutting Methods
  • Hand Stitching & Machine Stitching
  • Garment Construction – Blouse, Salwar, Kurta, Skirt
  • Embroidery & Embellishment Basics
  • Garment Finishing & Pressing Techniques
  • Alterations & Repair Work
  • Employability & Small Business Skills

Career Opportunities

Dress Maker / Tailor Boutique Owner Garment Factory Worker / Supervisor Alteration & Repair Specialist School/Office Uniform Contractor Home-Based Stitching Business

Placement Opportunities

Garment Export Units (Ahmedabad) Readymade Garment Shops School Uniform Manufacturers Bridal Wear Studios Hotel Uniform Suppliers Textile Export Companies

Future Scope & Career Path

Dress Making graduates have strong self-employment potential — opening a home boutique requires minimal investment and can generate a stable income from day one. Gujarat has one of India's largest textile and garment industries (Ahmedabad, Surat), creating continuous demand for skilled dress makers. Graduates who pursue Fashion Design & Technology or short courses in embroidery and machine embroidery can significantly increase their earning capacity. Bridal wear and custom clothing is a premium niche with very high per-garment income.
