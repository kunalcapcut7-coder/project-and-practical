Stenography English

1 Year NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹10,000–₹35,000/month

The Stenography English trade develops expert shorthand writing, dictation transcription, and professional typing skills in English. Stenographers are essential in courts, government offices, corporate boardrooms, and media organizations — a trade that remains highly relevant and well-paying in the digital age.

Syllabus / Subjects

Single-year intensive program. Speed targets: 80 WPM shorthand, 40 WPM typing by end of year.

  • English Shorthand – Pitman Method (Theory & Practice)
  • Shorthand Speed Building – 40 to 80 WPM
  • Transcription – Shorthand to Typewritten Text
  • English Typing – Manual & Computer Keyboard
  • Typing Speed Building – 30–40 WPM target
  • Office Practices & Secretarial Duties
  • Business English – Correspondence & Official Letters
  • MS Office – Word, Excel, PowerPoint
  • Court Procedures & Legal Documentation Basics
  • Employability & Communication Skills

Government Job Opportunities

Stenography graduates are eligible for direct government recruitment through multiple competitive exams:

SSC Stenographer Grade C & D Gujarat High Court Stenographer GPSSB / GPSC Clerical Cadre Railway Stenographer / Clerk

Future Scope & Career Path

Stenographers with high speed (100+ WPM) are highly valued in courts and government bureaucracy. The SSC Stenographer Grade C exam is one of the most popular government job exams. With experience, stenographers advance to Personal Assistant or Section Officer grade with excellent pay scales. Medical transcription is an emerging high-income niche where Steno graduates can upskill. Bilingual (English + Hindi/Gujarati) stenographers are even more sought after.
