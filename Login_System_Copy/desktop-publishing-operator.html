Desktop Publishing Operator

1 Year NCVT Certified 10th Pass ₹10,000–₹35,000/month

The Desktop Publishing (DTP) Operator trade trains students in graphic design, page layout, digital printing, and publishing workflows. Graduates work with professional software like Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and CorelDRAW to create newspapers, books, brochures, signboards, and digital media content.

Syllabus / Subjects

Intensive 1-year program covering both print and digital publishing workflows.

  • Graphic Design Fundamentals – Colour, Typography & Layout
  • Adobe Photoshop – Photo Editing & Retouching
  • CorelDRAW – Vector Graphics & Logo Design
  • Adobe Illustrator – Illustration & Artwork
  • Page Layout – Adobe InDesign / PageMaker
  • Printing Techniques – Offset, Digital & Screen Printing
  • Pre-press & PDF Generation for Print
  • Digital Media Design – Social Media & Web Banners
  • Typing Skills – English & Gujarati Fonts
  • Employability Skills & Client Communication

Future Scope & Career Path

DTP Operators who upskill into UI/UX design, motion graphics, or video editing can command significantly higher salaries in digital agencies. Freelancing is an excellent and immediate income option — logo design, brochure creation, and social media graphics yield ₹500–₹5,000 per project. With the rise of social media marketing, businesses constantly need skilled designers. Further learning in web design (HTML/CSS) or animation opens even broader career doors.
