Welder

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass High International Demand

The Welder trade provides training in Arc, Gas, TIG, and MIG welding techniques. Welders enjoy exceptionally high international demand — especially in UAE, Saudi Arabia, Germany, and Australia — in construction, shipbuilding, and oil & gas sectors.

Syllabus / Subjects

  • Workshop Calculation & Science
  • Engineering Drawing – Weld Symbols
  • Trade Theory – Arc & Gas Welding
  • Trade Practical – Basic Joint Welding
  • Safety & Employability Skills
  • Advanced Welding Theory – TIG/MIG
  • Trade Practical – Pipe & Structural Welding
  • Workshop Technology
  • Engineering Drawing – Fabrication Drawings
  • Project Work & Certification Preparation

Career Opportunities

Structural Welder Pipe Welder Fabrication Technician Quality Inspector Self-employed Welder Overseas Welder (Gulf/Europe)

Placement Opportunities

L&T ONGC Indian Railways BHEL Shipbuilding Companies Gulf & International Projects

Future Scope & Career Path

Welding is a trade with exceptional overseas earning potential. Obtaining AWS (American Welding Society) or ASME certifications can unlock opportunities in UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Germany with salaries of ₹60,000–₹1,20,000/month. Domestically, infrastructure expansion, the Make in India initiative, and defense manufacturing are creating thousands of new welding jobs. Senior welders can become Welding Inspectors or Quality Controllers with excellent career progression.
