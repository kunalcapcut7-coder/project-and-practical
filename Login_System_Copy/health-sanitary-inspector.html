Health Sanitary Inspector

2 Years NCVT Certified 10th Pass (Science) ₹15,000–₹40,000/month

The Health Sanitary Inspector (HSI) trade prepares students for the critical public health sector. Graduates are trained to inspect sanitation systems, enforce health regulations, conduct disease surveillance, manage waste disposal, and promote community health — serving in municipal corporations, hospitals, and government health departments.

  • Human Anatomy & Physiology Basics
  • Communicable & Non-Communicable Disease Control
  • Principles of Public Health & Community Hygiene
  • Water Supply & Sanitation Systems
  • Waste Management – Solid & Liquid Waste
  • Food Safety & Adulteration Inspection
  • Employability Skills
  • Environmental Health & Pollution Control
  • Epidemiology & Disease Surveillance
  • Maternal & Child Health Programs
  • National Health Programs – NHM, Swachh Bharat
  • Health Legislation & Inspection Procedures
  • Hospital Waste Management & Biohazard Safety
  • Field Training – Community Health Survey
  • Project Work – Public Health Awareness Campaign

Future Scope & Career Path

Public health has gained enormous importance post-COVID-19. Governments internationally are significantly increasing investments in sanitation, disease surveillance, and community health infrastructure. HSI graduates with ITI certification can appear for GPSSB Sanitary Inspector exams — one of the most stable government jobs in Gujarat. Further studies like Diploma or B.Sc. in Public Health or Environmental Science open pathways to senior health officer positions. International NGO and WHO-sponsored programs frequently recruit HSI-trained professionals for community health projects.
